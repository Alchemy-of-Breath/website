A note from Anthony

For most of my early life I lived inside a story I had not written. Privilege, success on the outside, a withering spirit on the inside. It took a serious illness in my fifties to bring me face to face with the question I had been avoiding: who am I, when the masks fall away?

What I found beneath the ashes of that old life became the work I have spent forty years refining. Seven chapters that walk you down into your own depths and back up again. The same map I have used to guide thousands of facilitators in our world-renowned training.

I have decided to open this map to anyone. This is a legacy offering. It cannot be reserved only for those who wish to become breathworkers. The journey home belongs to everyone who feels the call.

Anthony Abbagnano

Your subconscious mind was built in the first seven years of your life, when you were learning what it meant to be loved, to be safe, to be enough. Whatever happened in those years, and the ways you learned to react, became a script.

That script has been running ever since. It is the reason you keep choosing the same kind of partner. The reason your ambition stops at a certain ceiling. The reason you swallow what needs to be said.

It is not your fault. It happens to all of us. And the script can be rewritten, but it takes more than willpower. It takes meeting the parts of yourself that wrote it in the first place.

That meeting is what this journey is for.

Questions, gently

What you might be wondering.

I have no breathwork experience. Is this too advanced?+

No experience is needed. The journey is designed to meet you wherever you are. The early chapters open you gently, and the breath practices grow with you as you go. Many of our students arrive with no prior training and find this becomes one of the most transformative experiences of their lives.

Will the live sessions be recorded if I miss one?+

Yes. Every live session is recorded and stays available in your dashboard for the full year of access. We understand that life happens, and the work does not require perfect attendance to take root.

What does a week in the live cohort look like?+

One live class each week with the teacher, opening that week's chapter together. The cohort stays the same for the full seven weeks, so the group deepens as the work deepens. Between classes you have the workbook and reflection prompts. Every class is recorded and stays in your dashboard.

Is this only for people who want to become facilitators?+

No. This is the inner work that lives at the heart of our facilitator training, opened to anyone walking the journey for themselves. Many participants come purely for their own healing. Others find that after walking it, they want to continue into the facilitator training and bring this work to others.

How much time will it take each week?+

One ninety minute live class per week, plus two to three hours of reflection, workbook, and daily breath practice. The journey is designed to deepen, not overwhelm. Most participants find that the time it asks of them becomes time they look forward to.

What if I am working through something difficult right now?+

This work is trauma-informed, and the mentors and trainers are trained to hold what arises. That said, it is not a substitute for therapy. If you are in active mental health crisis, we recommend speaking with a professional first. You can also bring any concerns to your discovery call, and we will help you assess whether this is the right moment for you.
