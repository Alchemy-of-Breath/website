Questions and answers

Before you come

When exactly are the two weeks?

Intuition with Amy runs from Sunday, August 29 to Saturday, September 4, 2027. Alchemist with Anthony runs from Sunday, September 5 to Saturday, September 11, 2027. If you book both, you stay thirteen nights.

Can I come to just one week?

Yes. Each week is complete on its own, and you can also book the two together as one stay.

What does it cost?

Your price depends on the room you choose, and it is shown at checkout before you pay. Room, meals and all sessions are included. Travel is not.

Where is ASHA?

In Castell’Azzara, in the province of Grosseto, Southern Tuscany. A medieval hilltop village surrounded by Etruscan hills. ASHA covers forty two acres, with its own water and solar power.

How do I get there?

A rental car is strongly recommended, as the area is rural. By train, take the line to Chiusi-Chianciano Terme and then a taxi, roughly €100 to €120. Shared rides are usually arranged through the retreat WhatsApp group.

What is the food like?

All meals are plant based, freshly prepared from seasonal Tuscan produce by ASHA’s kitchen team. Gluten free and other dietary needs are accommodated, so please tell us in advance.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. You will be among people at every stage, from complete beginners to practitioners deepening work they already do. Come as you are.

What is the weather like?

Late August is usually between 18 and 27°C, warm in the day and cooler in the evenings. September can be more variable, so bring light layers, above all if you plan to camp.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs are welcome and must be kept on a leash to protect the resident cats. There is a €40 donation per pet per retreat.

Who do I contact with anything else?

The ASHA team, at info@asha.global or on WhatsApp at +39 353 452 7348.
