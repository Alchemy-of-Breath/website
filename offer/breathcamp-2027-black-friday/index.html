The retreat in one paragraph

What is BreathCamp?

BreathCamp is a 7-day, 6-night breathwork retreat at ASHA, built to help a busy, stretched nervous system slow down. It takes place at the Alchemy School of Healing Arts in southern Tuscany, Italy. Each day brings breathwork sessions, movement, workshops and time to rest, swim and explore the land, with three vegan meals a day. You are guided by Anthony Abbagnano, Amy Rachelle and our trainers, and you stay on site for the whole week, alongside people from around the world.

Do I need breathwork experience?

No. Anyone can join as a retreat guest. Your sessions cover how to breathe, the science of the breath and an inner journey session, so you can start from wherever you are.

Who else will be there?

Retreat guests and Alchemy of Breath facilitators completing their certification share the week. Each group follows a slightly different track.

Is it a training?

Not for retreat guests. BreathCamp is a retreat for you. If it sparks something, you can explore our Facilitator Training afterward.

Your guides

Who will guide the week?

Founders of Alchemy of Breath and of ASHA in Tuscany, together with our trainers.

Anthony Abbagnano

Founder and lead instructor

Anthony is a breathwork pioneer and the founder of Alchemy of Breath and ASHA. He founded the school in 2012 to close the gaps he saw in breathwork education: trainings that were too short, uneven standards, and too little trauma awareness. He is the author of Outer Chaos, Inner Calm, with more than 50 years of his own practice with the breath.

Amy Rachelle, ND

Co-founder and co-instructor

Amy is a naturopath and master facilitator with more than 15 years in breathwork, somatic practice and practitioner training. She blends breathwork, intuition, detox and voice into a warm, joyful way of working, and co-founded ASHA with Anthony in 2020.

Questions and answers

BreathCamp FAQ

How long is the Black Friday offer open?+
For the whole of Friday, November 27, 2026, until midnight in your own time zone. The timer on this page counts down to your local midnight.
Who can book the Black Friday offer?+
New members. Facilitator Training students and graduates have their own BreathCamp pricing and book through their graduate page. The offer cannot be combined with other promotions, price locks or scholarships.
What are the bonus Quantum Shift recordings?+
Two Quantum Shift recordings, Relationships and Beyond Forgiveness. They come with your BreathCamp booking when you book on Black Friday, and are sent to you after you book.
When is BreathCamp?+
The Black Friday offer covers two 2027 camps: Sunday, June 6 to Saturday, June 12, and Sunday, July 11 to Saturday, July 17.
Do I need breathwork experience?+
No. Anyone can join as a retreat guest, and your sessions start with how to breathe and the science of the breath.
What are arrival and departure like?+
Check-in is on Sunday from 2:30 pm. That afternoon you tour the property, join an opening ceremony with Anthony and Amy, and have your first breathwork session in the evening. The final session ends around 1:00 pm on Saturday, so plan your travel for after that.
Do I have to stay on site?+
Yes. Everyone stays at ASHA for the whole retreat.
What is included?+
Your accommodation for 7 days and 6 nights, three vegan meals a day, daily breathwork sessions and workshops, all programming and materials, and use of the pool and facilities. Flights, ground transport, travel insurance, individual wellbeing treatments and meals off site are not included. You can book wellbeing sessions in advance at asha.global/wellbeing-bookings. Sessions with Anthony and Amy fill quickly.
How much does it cost, and can I pay over time?+
With the Black Friday saving, prices run from €1,752 to €4,245 per person, depending on your room, or €7,225 for a private cottage for two. You can pay in full or set up a payment plan at checkout.
Can I share a room?+
Yes. If you book at the same time as a friend or partner, ask to share a room on the intake form.
Can I bring my child or my pet?+
Children under 10 stay free. Children 11 and over are charged with a 20% discount, in private accommodation only. Pets are welcome on a leash, with a 40 euro donation. ASHA has resident cats and dogs.
How do I get to ASHA?+
The nearest airport is Rome Fiumicino (FCO), about 2.5 hours from ASHA. Renting a car is the easiest option. By train, travel to Chiusi-Chianciano Terme station, where the local taxi service Il Postalino can bring you to ASHA for about €100 to €120 one way. Book it in advance. Travel is not included, and we recommend travel insurance.
Can I change or cancel my booking?+
We do not offer refunds, including for early departure. You can move to another camp in the same season and the same location with at least 45 days’ notice before your original start date. Email info@asha.global with the subject “DATE CHANGE REQUEST” and the date you would like. If we cancel a camp, we refund the full amount less currency exchange and bank fees.
Is breathwork safe for everyone?+
No. Breathwork is not suitable for everyone. Please read our Medical Disclaimer and Contraindications before you book.
