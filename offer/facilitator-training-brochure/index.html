Welcome

It starts with one breath

If you are holding this, something in you is already curious. Maybe breathwork has changed something for you, and you want to understand it more deeply. Maybe you sense you could hold that same space for other people. Maybe you simply want to know yourself better.

The Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Training was built for all three. It begins with you, with fifteen weeks of your own inner work, because the steadiness you bring into a room is the most important thing you will ever offer a client.

Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Training07
Part 1 · The Inner Journey

Fifteen weeks with your own story

Before you guide anyone else, you walk the path yourself. These are some of the questions students find themselves sitting with in Part 1.

I

"Where did my patterns really begin?"

You look honestly at the story you have been living, from your earliest experiences onward.

II

"What is asking to change in my life?"

You meet the part of you that is ready to move, and learn to take responsibility for that change.

III

"What does my younger self still need?"

Gentle work with the parts of you that learned to protect themselves early.

IV

"What have I been avoiding in myself?"

You learn to turn toward the uncomfortable parts with curiosity, rather than judgment.

V

"What gifts are hidden in my story?"

Experiences you carried as wounds often hold your deepest strengths as a facilitator.

VI

"What am I ready to let go of?"

Making peace with the past, so you can be fully present with whoever is in front of you.

VII

"Who am I bringing back with me?"

You come out of Part 1 steadier, clearer and more yourself. That is the ground every session you hold will stand on. Along the way: three sharing circles, masterclasses with Amy and Anthony, and the support of your cohort.

Questions, gently

What people often ask

Is this only for people who want to become facilitators?

No. Many people join for personal growth and healing. Others bring breathwork into coaching, therapy, bodywork, yoga or wellness work they already do.

I am already a practitioner. How does this help?

Breathwork offers a direct way to help clients release stored emotion and regulate their nervous system. Graduates often add workshops, private sessions and retreats to their practice.

What is Conscious Connected Breathwork?

Breathing with no pause between the inhale and the exhale, often with curated music. It calms the nervous system and helps release held emotion and limiting beliefs.

Why is the training eight months long?

A weekly rhythm gives you time between classes to integrate and practice what you learn. It fits around work and family, and it leaves room for real personal depth.

What does the 400 hours include?

Live classes, home study, reading and viewing, practice sessions and your graduation BreathCamp.

How much time will I need each week?

One live class, plus about 5 to 7 hours of home study: your breath practice, reflection, sharing with your study buddy and mentor, and the reading list.

Do I have to attend BreathCamp?

Yes. Facilitating a live session at BreathCamp is part of certifying for in-person work. In exceptional circumstances, an online certification may be possible. Speak with your trainer.

What support will I have?

Weekly live classes with Master Trainers, Q&A, personal feedback on your case studies, a study buddy and mentor, and your cohort.

When can I start facilitating?

You begin 1:1 practice sessions after the seventh seminar of Part 2 and group sessions after the eighth. You certify after your graduation BreathCamp.

How do I get started?

Book a free call with a Success Advisor. When you are ready, you enroll, and you get immediate access to the learning platform and preparation materials.
