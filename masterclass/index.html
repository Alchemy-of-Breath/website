A personal note from Anthony

What is the essence of true healing? This question has followed me my whole life, and one answer has kept presenting itself: the breath. Before I was twenty, I had found my way to conscious breathing and begun bringing it into my work. For more than fifty years, it has been the through-line of everything I do, in business rooms and classrooms, on the road with people in their hardest hours.

Alchemy of Breath was the first online breathwork school in the world. We are now one of the most thoroughly accredited and intensive institutes anywhere, having reached millions of people and certified over a thousand facilitators.

But it has never been about the numbers.

The breath belongs to no one. My aim is for everyone to share its power with integrity, safety, and a compassionate heart. I know what it is to feel uncertain, powerless, passive. To hope that one day it will all get better while suffering in silence.

What is left of my work is to make sure no one has to do that alone.

Anthony Abbagnano
Founder, Alchemy of Breath

Before you sign up

A few honest answers.

Is the masterclass really free?+

Yes. We will send the masterclass link to your inbox, along with the occasional breath-related note. You can unsubscribe with one tap.

Do I need any breathwork experience?+

No. The masterclass is built for the first-timer as much as the long-time practitioner. Anthony begins gently, and the practice meets you wherever you are.

How long does it take?+

About 90 minutes. The guided breath itself is around 40 minutes, with Anthony's framework and questions woven around it.

Is it live or recorded?+

You can watch the recording whenever the hour finds you. The masterclass is yours to keep for as long as you want it.

What if I have anxiety, asthma, or a heart condition?+

Conscious connected breathing is safe for most people, and many begin this work precisely because of anxiety. If you are managing a serious heart condition, severe asthma, recent surgery, or pregnancy, please check with your doctor first. The masterclass includes guidance on adjusting the practice for your body.

Do I have to commit to anything after?+

No. The masterclass is complete in itself. If something opens up and you want to walk further, we will share what is possible. No pressure either way.
