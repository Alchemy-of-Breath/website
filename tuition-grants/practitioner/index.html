Who is the Practitioner Pathway Grant for?

Existing practitioners who work with clients, including yoga and movement teachers, coaches, therapists, bodyworkers, nurses, doulas, and fitness trainers.

Which program does it support?

The grant supports the eight-month online Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Training, including personal inner work and professional facilitation learning.

How does the tuition grant work?

Receive a 30% tuition grant when you pay in full, or a 25% tuition grant when you choose a payment plan. Your advisor will explain tuition, the payment schedule, and the grant before you decide.

How can I use breathwork in my current practice?

The training develops skills for guiding individual and group breathwork and explores breathwork as a business. How you integrate it depends on your qualifications, professional scope, and the people you support.

Does the grant itself provide certification?

Certification requires completion of the full training and its requirements. Your advisor can explain the learning, practice, and graduation pathway on your call.
