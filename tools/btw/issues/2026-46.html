Dear friend,

Do you believe there might be a higher purpose to everything you’re going through right now — and everything you’ve been through?

Yet… what do we do with all the turmoil?

We find it in the world, in our relationships, and perhaps most importantly… within ourselves.

So often, we resist change. We avoid it, fight it, or desperately try to escape it.

But what if, instead, we accepted it? Embraced it? Even welcomed it as medicine?

It may taste bitter at first, but what if it’s exactly what the doctor ordered — even when we don’t like it?

You know what they say: “No friction, no flow.” And, of course, “No rain, no rainbow.”

Yet when we’re caught in the thick of turmoil, it can feel like there’s no way out. We’re trapped. Stuck. Powerless.

And that’s when those four dangerous words creep in:

“I have no choice.”

Really? Is that actually true?

Stop. Breathe. Reframe.

Perhaps we don’t have a choice about what’s happening, but we often have a choice in how we meet it.

Change is happening anyway. What if we stopped insisting life should be different and began working with what is? Even when it breaks our hearts into a million pieces.

Those four little words — I have no choice — can undermine and determine the direction of our lives. They can keep us feeling “safe,” watching life pass by — rather than stepping out and actually living it.

Because being FULLY in life can feel:

  • Uncertain
  • Turbulent
  • Messy
  • Scary
  • ALIVE!

And holding ourselves back can leave us feeling:

  • Fearful
  • Insecure
  • Stuck
  • Disconnected and dysregulated
  • NOT FULLY ALIVE

So, is change moving through your life right now? What if it’s not here to break you, but to break something open within you?

What if accepting change with grace opens doors you can’t yet see?

And what if the turmoil itself becomes the catalyst for your greatest transformation?

Perhaps the invitation isn’t to wait until life feels safe to live fully, but to discover that we can feel fully alive even in the uncertainty.
