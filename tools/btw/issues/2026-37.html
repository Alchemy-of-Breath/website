Dear friend,

What rises in you when you read those words… building the life you love?

Does it feel possible? Does it send you soaring into your own higher vision?

Or does something else show up — disbelief, doubt, discouragement?

Whatever you believe, you’re right. And in the way life works — we get exactly what we believe we are… worth.

Why does one person, disadvantaged though determined, build the life they love — while another, handed every advantage, doesn’t? Who’s actually the disadvantaged one?

One refused to stop because a fire burned inside. They took the risk — often because they don’t have any other resources — and thereby discovered their worth and capability. While the other doesn’t have the impetus to do anything different, and their true worth remains unchallenged.

No risk = no reward.

Do you believe your dreams are actually possible?

Will it be a risk? For sure.

Will your self-worth be in question? Probably.

What if it’s this simple? Take the risk, let go of needing to be too comfortable, discover your true worth — and build the life you love, instead of settling for what’s just… ok?

See it. Believe it. Do it.

Belief only takes us so far though. At some point we need to act on our worth. What we believe is what we value. What we value determines the quality of life we live.

What does it actually mean to live the life you love?
