Dear friend,

What defines success for you?

And if you’ve already tasted society’s version of it, what does true success mean to you now?

For so many of us, in the pursuit of happiness or whatever our version of success looks like, the fear of failure lives right on our heels. It’s a double edged sword. It can motivate us in a good way, or it can haunt us.

Driving, pushing, head too full, neglecting our bodies and our true needs, neglecting the people we love because we don’t have time for them. All for “success”?

It’s a highly addictive place to live in. Getting some success, then needing more, becomes the driver.

Success addiction is dopamine chasing dopamine: your brain rewards the pursuit of the next win, not the having of it, so each achievement resets the bar and leaves you emptier, not fuller.

Fear of failure is just this same circuit in reverse — avoiding the dopamine crash of loss, not protecting anything real. True success is the nervous system no longer needing the hit: regulated enough to act from purpose instead of from the next fix.

We’ve all been heavily conditioned, either to chase it or to rebel against the status quo.

That was my story. Rebel against what society said, against the norm, “rage against the machine.” Little did I understand at the time that I was only fighting myself. Success? It’s definitely not how I define it now.

Success, for me now, is about freedom. Turning my fear of failure into freedom.

Anthony is a master of this.

He has experienced a tremendous amount of what the world calls “success.”

Wealth, acclaim, power. But by his late 40s, he found himself wondering what it all actually meant, because underneath it all was a kind of emptiness. Not the empty, quiet mind you find in meditation. The other kind. Full of dissatisfaction, no matter how much he’d achieved in the world’s eyes.

That’s where he began learning how to turn the fear of failure into his greatest freedom.

That’s his story, and I’ll leave it to him to share it with you this Sunday.

Have a wonderful Sunday with this gem of a human, and perhaps consider what true success looks like for you, and what that means in your life right now.

Love and support you beautiful human you,
