The training in one paragraph

What is the 21-day live residential breathwork facilitator training?

It is a breathwork facilitator certification training, lived in person. It teaches you to guide people through Conscious Connected Breathwork safely, 1:1 and in groups. You spend 21 days living and training at ASHA in Tuscany, breathing and facilitating every day under the direct supervision of Anthony Abbagnano, Amy Rachelle and our Master Trainers. Then you continue online for 4 months, with follow-up live classes and mentorship. The Seeds of Alchemy workshop and the Inner Journey are included too, and you can take the Inner Journey before or after the residential, or at your own pace. There is no separate BreathCamp to attend. You finish with the same 400-hour Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Certification, at the same standard and with the same accreditation as the online path.

Is it the same certification as the online training?

Yes. Both paths lead to the same 400-hour Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Certification, accredited in the same way. What differs is how the learning is delivered. Certification is not automatic on either path: it is awarded when you show you are ready to hold the work safely.

Why learn in person?

Holding a room is something you learn in a room. At ASHA you practice with real people every day, receive feedback from your trainers straight away, and feel the work in your body before you guide anyone else through it.

Starting rates are conservative figures taken from prices breathwork facilitators publish in your region. Change any of them to what practitioners near you charge.

Where these rates come from
  • 1:1 sessions. Published US practitioner rates for a 60 to 90 minute session commonly sit between $85 and $150, for example $150 a session (Metamorphose Breathwork). UK listings run from about £50 to £75, German and Austrian listings from about €110 to €150, and Australian listings from about A$140 to A$250. One industry overview puts the wider range at $50 to $275 an hour (WeShare, 2026).
  • Group workshops. US group classes are listed at about $35 to $50 per person, for example $50 a session (Metamorphose Breathwork). Toronto studio drop-ins run about C$45 to C$75, London workshops about £40, and Amsterdam group experiences €50 and up. The starting figure assumes about ten people, before any venue share.
  • Corporate sessions. Published one-hour team sessions cluster between about $300 and $600, for example $500 for 60 minutes in Arizona (Liquid Breathwork) and A$320 for an hour in Australia (ClassBento). A European listing shows €290 for 30 minutes.
  • Retreats. Few facilitators publish guest fees, so this starts low. Published workshop facilitation fees range from about $500 to $2,000.
  • Training others. Published facilitator trainings of 200 to 350 hours are commonly priced between about $3,000 and $7,000 per student.
  • Employed breathwork instructors earn less per hour, about $21 on average in the US (ZipRecruiter, 2026). These figures are for facilitators running their own practice.

Questions and answers

Residential training FAQ

How long does it take to get certified?+
You spend 21 days in person at ASHA, then continue online for 4 months with follow-up live classes and mentorship. You also complete the Inner Journey, before or after the residential, or at your own pace. Certification is awarded when you show you are ready to hold the work safely.
Do I need prior breathwork experience?+
No. The training is designed for every level of experience. An introductory module guides you through the foundations of breathwork. What matters most is your willingness to do your own inner work.
What is Conscious Connected Breathwork?+
A technique where the inhale and exhale flow without pauses, often with specially curated music. It calms the nervous system, supports the parasympathetic response, and helps release held emotion and limiting beliefs.
What certification will I receive, and what can I offer with it?+
You receive the Alchemy of Breath Facilitator Certification. It prepares you to facilitate 1:1 sessions, group workshops and breath journeys in a safe, trauma informed way, both online and in person. Breathwork facilitation is not a licensed profession, and the certification is not a medical credential.
How much does the training cost?+
Tuition is $7,200, the same as the online Facilitator Training, and is paid to Alchemy of Breath. Super early bird discounts are available, so book a call to see which discount is active right now. You can pay with a deposit followed by monthly installments. Accommodation and meals at ASHA are booked separately, are priced in euros and depend on the room you choose. Our team will go through the options with you on your call, so you can see the full cost before you decide.
When are the next dates?+
The next residential runs from Sunday, June 20 to Saturday, July 10, 2027. Places are limited, and a call with our team is required before enrolling.
Where will I stay, and what is ASHA like?+
You live at ASHA, the Alchemy School of Healing Arts, for the full 21 days. It is a retreat and community center on 15 hectares at the edge of a medieval village in southern Tuscany, with forest, olive groves, gardens, a pool and open-air practice spaces. Rooms range from your own tent to a private cottage, from €1,649 for the full residential, with three vegan meals a day included.
How do I get to ASHA?+
Fly into Rome Fiumicino (FCO). ASHA is about 2.5 hours away, and renting a car is the easiest option. By train, travel to Chiusi-Chianciano Terme station, where the local taxi service Il Postalino can bring you to ASHA for about €100 to €120 one way. Book it in advance. Travel is not included, and we recommend travel insurance.
What is included in my stay?+
Your room and three vegan meals a day for the full 21 days, plus the pool, gardens and practice spaces. Dietary needs and allergies are cared for. Massage, sound healing and private sessions with Anthony or Amy can be booked on site, and fees apply. Wifi is available in the main house and the Dharma Den, not in the rooms.
Do I need to stay for the full 21 days?+
Yes. Students stay at ASHA and attend the full 21 days to be eligible for certification. If you would like to arrive early or stay on afterward, arrange it directly with ASHA at info@asha.global.
Do I need to attend BreathCamp as well?+
No. Your 21 days at ASHA are your in-person training, so there is no separate BreathCamp to book.
What is the refund policy?+
We do not offer refunds, and your place is tied to the dates you book. Please choose the residential that works for you, and talk it through with our team on your call.
Is breathwork safe for everyone?+
No. Breathwork is not suitable for everyone. Please read our Medical Disclaimer and Contraindications before you enroll.
How much time will I need?+
The 21 days at ASHA are your full focus. A typical day includes morning practice, breathwork sessions and workshops, group sharing, meals and some free time, with evening events. After that, you continue online for 4 months with follow-up classes and mentorship.
Is this only for people who want to become facilitators?+
Not at all. Many people join for personal growth and healing. Others add breathwork to coaching, therapy, bodywork, yoga or wellness work they already do.
I am already a practitioner. How does this help my work?+
Breathwork offers a direct way to help clients release stored emotion and regulate their nervous system. Graduates often deepen their existing sessions and expand into workshops, private breath sessions and retreats.
What happens on the Success Advisor call?+
A free, 45 minute call. Your advisor answers your questions, talks with you about where you are, and helps you decide whether the training is the right fit. There is no obligation to enroll.
