Getting there

Find your way to ASHA.

Put this in your navigator:
Località Le Piane, 01030, Italy

We highly recommend renting a car if you are arriving by plane. It's easier, and may be less expensive when shared with other guests. Post in your camp's WhatsApp group for a ride share — watch your email to join the group after signing up.

Taxi service from the train is available at about 100+ euros per trip.
