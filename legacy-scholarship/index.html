Questions, gently

A few honest answers.

Who can apply?+

Anyone who feels called to become a breathwork facilitator and for whom the cost of the training is a real barrier. No particular background is required. What matters is the calling and a clear sense of who you want to serve.

What is the difference between the two paths?+

The online path is eight months of study from wherever you are, finishing with one week at BreathCamp on the land at ASHA for your graduation, which is paid separately. The residential path is twenty-one days living the practice at ASHA in Tuscany, followed by four months of study and mentorship online. Both lead to the same triple accredited certification, and the scholarship applies to either.

What does the scholarship cover?+

A meaningful part of your tuition, awarded at 25%, 35%, or 50% of the $7,200 fee, up to $3,600. It is a partial scholarship rather than a full ride, so there is still an investment from you. Your level is decided together with your Success Advisor.

Is it based on financial need?+

Need and fit both matter. The interview is where we come to understand both, so we can place each award where it will do the most good.

What happens in the interview?+

An honest conversation about what is calling you, who you hope to serve, your circumstances, and whether the timing is right. Nothing to prepare. Just come as you are.

When will I hear back?+

Applications close Wednesday, August 5 at 9pm UK time. We hold the interview calls after that, and you will hear back from us by August 14 at the latest, whether the answer is yes or not this time.

What if I am not chosen this time?+

You are still welcome here. We will point you toward other ways in, from free sessions to payment options on the training, and you are welcome to apply again when the doors open next.
