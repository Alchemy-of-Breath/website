The science of transformation

Our brain uses 20-25% of our oxygen supply as nourishment. But in today’s world with pollution, air-conditioned environments, and less exercise, we are not getting what we need. The result is dimmed mindful awareness, but we have gotten so used to it that we think of it as normal. We accept diminished vitality as our baseline, and our brain learns to live with less.

So what could happen if we increased the dosage? With the Alchemy of Breath, we increase the supply of oxygen to the brain. We awaken the system again, and through our connected breath pattern, we not only restore our brain with what it craves, but much more, too. For those that have meditated before, you will find the experience a powerful way to support your practice. For those who know they should meditate, but don’t, the Alchemy of Breath will provide you with an instant opportunity to connect to the benefits of deep meditation.

Meet your guide:Anthony Abbagnano

Alchemy of Breath and ASHA Founder • Author • Global Breathwork Pioneer

Anthony Abbagnano is the visionary founder of Alchemy of Breath, a globally recognized breathwork community that has transformed thousands of lives through the power of conscious breathing, and ASHA (Alchemy School of Healing Arts in Tuscany, Italy)

As the first person to bring breathwork online, Anthony serves as curator for online breathwork on the board of the Global Professional Breathwork Alliance (GPBA) and holds a faculty position on the prestigious Shift Network.

Anthony has shared stages with world-renowned leaders including Deepak Chopra, Marianne Williamson, Bruce Lipton, Peter Levine, Wim Hof, Fleet Maull, and even President Joe Biden. He is the author of “Outer Chaos, Inner Calm,” a groundbreaking guide to reclaiming inner peace through breathwork, personal transformation, and mindful awareness.

His mission is to awaken the entire world to the miraculous gift of conscious breath through the Breathe The World free sessions, BreathCamps at ASHA (Alchemy School of Healing Arts in Tuscany, Italy), and by training the master facilitators who will carry this life-changing work to every corner of the globe.

Meet your guide:Amy Rachelle, ND

Alchemy of Breath and ASHA Co-Founder • Naturopath • Intuitive Healer

Amy Rachelle, ND is the co-founder of Alchemy of Breath and ASHA (Alchemy School of Healing Arts in Tuscany, Italy). With her decades of experience and unique intuitive wisdom, she brings profound insight and vision to every aspect of Alchemy of Breath’s offerings.

As both a licensed naturopathic doctor specializing in food as medicine and detoxification, and a world-renowned medical medium and intuitive healer, Amy’s approach bridges ancient healing wisdom with modern medical understanding.

She’s also a teacher, singer, performer, writer, and public speaker. Her mission is to help people discover the potency and power they carry within, moving through negative self-talk and trauma to activate their greatest potential, because “the world needs your gifts, more than ever.”

Anthony & Amy’s mission

Healing humanity one breath, one person, one community at a time, and proving that when we lift each other up instead of tearing each other down, we can create the harmonious existence our world desperately needs.
