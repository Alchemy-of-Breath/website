A welcome from Anthony and Amy

We created BreathCamp for the moments when a person knows something needs care, even if they do not yet know what comes next. Over many years, we have welcomed people from every walk of life, people at an ending, a beginning, a crossroads, or simply tired of carrying too much alone.

We built ASHA as a place where all of you is welcome. There is no need to arrive with a plan for who you will become. There is room to breathe, rest, be witnessed, and hear your own life more clearly.

Come and set ordinary life down for seven days. Let the land, the practice, and the people offer the kind of support that makes a new step feel possible.

Anthony & Amy
Founders, Alchemy of Breath and ASHA

Day seven: Take it home07

Leave with practices, people, and a clearer relationship to the life waiting for you when you return home.

Questions, answered honestly

Everything practical, in plain words.

I am very new to breathwork. Is BreathCamp right for me?+

You are welcome exactly where you are. You will be surrounded by graduates whose whole gift is meeting people just like you, many breathing consciously for the first time. The content and care hold both the experienced and the brand new. You will be looked after from your very first session.

Is there a payment plan?+

Yes. A 20% deposit secures your stay, then you pay the balance in monthly installments, fully paid two weeks before arrival. Choose "Deposit with installment payments" at checkout.

What does the €888 place include, and what do I book separately?+

The 888 euro place covers the full seven-day program: all breathwork sessions, workshops, rituals, and ceremony. Your room and daily meals are booked directly with ASHA after you reserve your retreat place.

Can I bring a friend or my partner?+

Yes, and their place is 50% off. After you reserve, you will receive a discount to add a friend or partner to the week.

Which airport should I fly into?+

Rome Fiumicino (FCO) is best. From there you can rent a car, which is the easiest option, or take the train to Chiusi-Chianciano Terme. Once you are signed up, your community forum helps you coordinate rides.

Is there a taxi service to ASHA?+

From the train at Chiusi-Chianciano Terme, the local taxi Il Postalino can bring you to ASHA. Book in advance: ilpostalino.com, WhatsApp +39 335 5424668. Roughly 100 to 120 euros one way, English and Italian spoken.

Can I leave early?+

If you cannot stay the full seven days, you are welcome to leave early, though we cannot offer a refund or discount. Students attending to complete the Facilitator Training need to stay the full week to graduate.

What is the daily schedule, and what time do we finish?+

A typical day runs from morning movement and breakfast, through breathwork and workshops, a group share, lunch, free time, dinner, and an evening gathering. A detailed schedule is sent as your date approaches. On the final day we finish after lunch, around 2 pm.

Can I bring my children?+

Children are welcome and can stay in private accommodation with their guardian (tent, glamping, camper, or private cottage), not in shared rooms. Under 10 stay and eat free. Ages 11 and up are charged at a 20% discount. Email info@asha.global for the family coupon and details.

What should I bring?+

Loose, layered clothing for sessions and house slippers, long pants and sturdy walking shoes, a swimsuit, warm layers for the evenings, biodegradable toiletries, an alarm clock, bug spray, and ear plugs if you are noise-sensitive. We recommend your own yoga mat, and travel and medical insurance.

What about alcohol and other substances?+

ASHA is a well-being center, so this is not the place for smoking, drugs, or alcohol. A glass of wine here and there is fine, in keeping with the nature of the work and the local culture. Smoking is only permitted in the parking lot.

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in is Sunday from 2:30 pm to 5:00 pm, with orientation at 4:30 pm, so please arrive in time. Check-out is Saturday from 8:00 am to 9:30 am, and the program ends with lunch around 2 pm. We suggest staying until the very end.

Do all guests stay on-site?+

Yes. Staying off-site is not available, and BreathCamp is a residential week, so the community stays together throughout.

Can I choose who I share a room with?+

Often, yes. Book at the same time and note your request on the intake form. For last-minute bookings we may not be able to accommodate the request.

Is there wifi?+

Free wifi is available in the main house and the Dharma Den, though not in the rooms or across the property. Part of the gift of the week is stepping back from the everyday.

Can I bring my pet?+

ASHA is home to seven cats and two dogs. Dogs are welcome on a leash, which is strictly enforced, with a 40 euro donation per pet. Please clean up after them and be mindful of the resident animals.

Can I reserve now and pay later?+

Because of high demand, a reservation is only confirmed once payment or a deposit has been made. We are not able to hold rooms.

What is the refund policy?+

We do not offer refunds, and bookings are non-transferable between people, though attending a different date may be possible depending on availability. Should we cancel, we refund the full amount minus currency exchange and bank fees. We recommend travel insurance, and ask that you read the full terms before booking.

What private well-being sessions are available?+

Life Direction Coaching and private breathwork with Anthony, intuitive readings with Amy, sound healing, massage, and more. Space with Anthony and Amy is limited, so book as early as you can.

How do I book a well-being session?+

Contact reception at info@asha.global and the team will help you, or book at ASHA when you arrive. These sessions carry their own fee, separate from your retreat place.

How do I get to town without a car?+

It is about 1.5 kilometers, a beautiful 20 minute walk up the hill along the valley view, so best for the fit-minded, or a 5 minute drive.

Where can I do laundry, and where can I shop?+

There is a laundromat in the center of Castell'Azzara, a 20 minute walk under the arches. For groceries there is the Co-op, a veg store, and a Thursday morning market. We recommend bringing any specialty items.

What is there to do nearby?+

Many hiking trails, the beautiful villages of Pitigliano and Sorano, Etruscan historical sites, and several natural hot springs within half an hour, including Bagni San Filippo. There are lovely local restaurants in Castell'Azzara and San Giovanni delle Contee.
