Questions

What you might be wondering.

Will the sessions be recorded if I miss it?+
Yes, the sessions will be recorded. In the event of a necessary absence, the number of missed hours will have to be taken again by the viewing of the recording of the course coupled with pertinent exercise participation.
I’m new to breathwork. Is this for me?+
Absolutely. This course is for beginner to a competent facilitator. There will also be breathwork sessions in the course.
Is there support outside of class?+
You will get access to your small mentor group, bi-weekly sharing circle, as well as bi-weekly Inner Journey support group with Anthony. You will also be included in the Inner Journey student forum on our platform.
What is the difference between the morning and evening class?+
Nothing but the time of day. Both classes cover the same Module 1 material, live, with the same access to the materials afterwards. Choose whichever fits your schedule and time zone.
