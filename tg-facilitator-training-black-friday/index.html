Black Friday FAQ

How long is the Black Friday offer open?+
For the whole of Saturday, November 28, 2026, until midnight in your own time zone. The timer on this page counts down to your local midnight.
What does it cost?+
$5,040 instead of $7,200, a saving of $2,160. The price is the same for the online training and for the Live Residential tuition.
Should I pay in full or pay a deposit?+
Paying in full locks in $5,040 and settles your tuition in one step. If you would rather start with a deposit, it is $500 for the online training and $997 for the Live Residential, and it holds the $5,040 price.
Can I talk to someone before I buy?+
Yes. Book a free call with a Success Advisor any day before Saturday. On Black Friday itself, checkout is direct, so this week is the time to ask your questions.
What is the difference between online and residential?+
The online training runs for 8 months, with weekly live calls on Zoom, and ends with a graduation BreathCamp at ASHA in Tuscany, booked separately. The Live Residential is 21 days in person at ASHA, June 20 to July 10, 2027, plus 4 months online, with accommodation booked separately. Both lead to the same 400-hour certification.
When does the online cohort start?+
The Black Friday price is for T35, starting Tuesday, February 16, 2027. Live calls are on Tuesdays at 7:00 pm EST, which also suits Australia, or 5:30 pm UK time.
Who can use the offer?+
New students. The offer is not for anyone already enrolled.
When is the live Q&A?+
Saturday, November 28, at 9:00 am Pacific, live with Amy and Anthony. Save your seat with the button in the Q&A section.
Is breathwork safe for everyone?+
No. Breathwork is not suitable for everyone. Please read our disclaimer, and talk to your Success Advisor or doctor if you have any health concerns.
